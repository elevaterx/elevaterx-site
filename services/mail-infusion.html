What the engagement examines

Strategic fit, financial feasibility, and build requirements.

01 / DEMAND

Opportunity and referral base

Assess available demand indicators, current referral patterns, service mix, geography, and the data limitations that affect confidence.

02 / ECONOMICS

Scenario-based feasibility

Model volume ramp, revenue, acquisition and delivery cost, labor, non-labor expense, capital, and timing under explicit assumptions.

03 / OPERATIONS

Staffing and space requirements

Translate the selected scenario into role mix, loaded labor, hours, room-by-room needs, circulation, and preliminary footprint fit.

04 / SEQUENCE

Implementation path

Identify prerequisites, decision gates, owners, dependencies, and the order in which capabilities should be developed.

Scope boundary

Feasibility is not facility design.

Space programming and schematic layouts support early decisions. A licensed architect or engineer produces construction documents, validates code requirements, and completes the buildable design.
