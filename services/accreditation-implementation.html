When this is useful

Accreditation work becomes manageable when the evidence and workplan agree.

Readiness

The starting point is unclear

Policies and operating practices exist, but the team lacks a consistent view of which standards are met, partial, absent, or unsupported.

Execution

Gaps lack owners and sequence

A checklist identifies issues but does not show dependencies, responsible parties, target dates, or how work affects launch readiness.

Sustainment

Evidence needs to remain current

Policies, training, records, quality activities, and supporting artifacts need an organized renewal and review discipline after the initial survey.

What the engagement can include

A readiness assessment connected to an implementation plan.

01 / MAP

Standards-to-policy mapping

Organize existing policies and supporting evidence against the selected accreditation framework, preserving citations and human review.

02 / TRIAGE

Gap and readiness analysis

Classify gaps, distinguish documentation from operational deficits, and identify the items that materially affect launch or survey readiness.

03 / BUILD

Policy readiness and manual support

Define the required policy set, source requirements from primary authorities, and structure clean-room policy content for controlled customization.

04 / EXECUTE

Implementation roadmap

Convert gaps and launch requirements into phases, tasks, owners, durations, predecessors, and dates that cascade from the target timeline.

What you receive

A practical route from current state to readiness.

  • Accreditation gap and readiness report
  • Policy and evidence crosswalk
  • Prioritized remediation register
  • Editable implementation plan
  • Phase-level timeline and dependencies
  • Leadership and project-team reporting cuts
Human review stays central

AI can accelerate the first pass, not make the accreditation decision.

Policy matches and draft findings require expert confirmation. The accreditor determines compliance; the work product organizes evidence, exposes gaps, and directs preparation.
