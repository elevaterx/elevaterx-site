What the work examines

From prescription opportunity to operating economics.

01 / SIZE

Opportunity sizing

Translate available prescribing data into specialty and retail opportunity, drug and class mix, destination patterns, payer mix, and first-fill economics.

02 / FORECAST

Pharmacy pro forma

Model ramp, reimbursement, acquisition cost, staffing, non-labor expense, capital, current-state baseline, and contribution over time.

03 / PRESSURE TEST

Scenarios and sensitivities

Show how capture, pricing, cost, timing, investment, and engagement structure change the result rather than hiding uncertainty in one forecast.

04 / DECIDE

Valuation, budget, and approval views

Support acquisition valuation, conservative portfolio budgeting, internal deal review, and a client-facing case without mixing their purposes.

Model discipline

A forecast is not a promise.

The useful model is the one that shows what is known, what is inferred, what is assumed, and what would change the decision. Outputs should be refreshed when material inputs change.
